A panel that slides in from an edge of the screen and can be swiped away, built on the Base UI
`Drawer`. It opens from the bottom by default, and supports side drawers and snap points. For a
non-swipeable side panel, use `Sheet`.

<Example>

    <DrawerContent>

        <DrawerDescription>Set your daily activity goal.</DrawerDescription>

## Direction

`down` (bottom, the default), `up` (top), `left` or `right`.

## Snap points

`snapPoints` lets a vertical drawer rest at several heights. Numbers from 0 to 1 are fractions of
the viewport height, larger numbers are pixels, and strings accept `px` or `rem`.

<Example>
  <Drawer snapPoints={[0.5, 1]} defaultSnapPoint={0.5} showSwipeHandle>
    <DrawerTrigger render={<Button variant="outline" />}>Open snap drawer</DrawerTrigger>
    <DrawerContent>
      <DrawerHeader>
        <DrawerTitle>Activity</DrawerTitle>
        <DrawerDescription>Drag up to expand, or down to dismiss.</DrawerDescription>
      </DrawerHeader>
      <DrawerFooter>
        <DrawerClose render={<Button variant="outline" />}>Close</DrawerClose>
      </DrawerFooter>
    </DrawerContent>
  </Drawer>


## Parts

- `Drawer` is the root. It holds the open state, direction and snap points.
- `DrawerTrigger` opens the drawer. Use `render={<Button />}` to style it.
- `DrawerContent` is the popup, rendered in a portal with a backdrop (when `modal`).
- `DrawerHeader`, `DrawerTitle` and `DrawerDescription` make up the header. Always include a `DrawerTitle`.
- `DrawerFooter` lays out the actions at the bottom.
- `DrawerClose` closes the drawer. It also accepts `render`.

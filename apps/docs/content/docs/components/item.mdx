A flexible row for displaying content: an optional `ItemMedia`, an `ItemContent` with an
`ItemTitle` and `ItemDescription`, and optional `ItemActions`. Use it for settings rows, list
entries, notifications and other repeated content. This is the shadcn `Item` component.

<Example>
  <Box width="100%" maxWidth="420px">
    <Item variant="outline">
      <ItemContent>
        <ItemTitle>Two-factor authentication</ItemTitle>
        <ItemDescription>Require a code from your phone when you sign in.</ItemDescription>
      </ItemContent>
      <ItemActions>
        <Button variant="outline" size="sm">Enable</Button>
      </ItemActions>
    </Item>
  </Box>

  ```jsx
  <Item variant="outline">
    <ItemContent>
      <ItemTitle>Two-factor authentication</ItemTitle>
      <ItemDescription>Require a code from your phone when you sign in.</ItemDescription>
    </ItemContent>
    <ItemActions>
      <Button variant="outline" size="sm">Enable</Button>
    </ItemActions>
  </Item>
  ```
</Example>

## Variants

`default` has no border or background, `outline` adds a border and `muted` a subtle background.

<Example>
  <Column gap="4" width="100%" maxWidth="420px">
    <Item>
      <ItemContent>
        <ItemTitle>Default</ItemTitle>
        <ItemDescription>No border or background.</ItemDescription>
      </ItemContent>
    </Item>
    <Item variant="outline">
      <ItemContent>
        <ItemTitle>Outline</ItemTitle>
        <ItemDescription>A border around the item.</ItemDescription>
      </ItemContent>
    </Item>
    <Item variant="muted">
      <ItemContent>
        <ItemTitle>Muted</ItemTitle>
        <ItemDescription>A subtle background.</ItemDescription>
      </ItemContent>
    </Item>
  </Column>

  ```jsx
  <Item>...</Item>
  <Item variant="outline">...</Item>
  <Item variant="muted">...</Item>
  ```
</Example>

## Media

Put an icon, avatar or image in `ItemMedia`. The `icon` variant sizes an icon, and the `image`
variant crops an image to a square.

<Example>
  <Box width="100%" maxWidth="420px">
    <Item variant="outline">
      <ItemMedia variant="icon">
        <Lucide.ShieldAlert />
      </ItemMedia>
      <ItemContent>
        <ItemTitle>Security alert</ItemTitle>
        <ItemDescription>A new device signed in to your account.</ItemDescription>
      </ItemContent>
      <ItemActions>
        <Button size="sm">Review</Button>
      </ItemActions>
    </Item>
  </Box>

  ```jsx
  <Item variant="outline">
    <ItemMedia variant="icon">
      <ShieldAlert />
    </ItemMedia>
    <ItemContent>
      <ItemTitle>Security alert</ItemTitle>
      <ItemDescription>A new device signed in to your account.</ItemDescription>
    </ItemContent>
    <ItemActions>
      <Button size="sm">Review</Button>
    </ItemActions>
  </Item>
  ```
</Example>

## As a link

Use `render` to render the item as another element, such as a link. Links get a hover background.

<Example>
  <Box width="100%" maxWidth="420px">
    <Item variant="outline" render={<a href="#as-a-link" />}>
      <ItemContent>
        <ItemTitle>Account settings</ItemTitle>
        <ItemDescription>Manage your profile, email and password.</ItemDescription>
      </ItemContent>
      <ItemActions>
        <Lucide.ChevronRight className="size-4" />
      </ItemActions>
    </Item>
  </Box>

  ```jsx
  <Item variant="outline" render={<a href="/settings" />}>
    <ItemContent>
      <ItemTitle>Account settings</ItemTitle>
      <ItemDescription>Manage your profile, email and password.</ItemDescription>
    </ItemContent>
    <ItemActions>
      <ChevronRight className="size-4" />
    </ItemActions>
  </Item>
  ```
</Example>

## Group

Wrap items in an `ItemGroup` to list them, with an `ItemSeparator` between them.

<Example>
  <Box width="100%" maxWidth="420px" border borderRadius="lg">
    <ItemGroup>
      <Item>
        <ItemMedia variant="icon">
          <Lucide.Mail />
        </ItemMedia>
        <ItemContent>
          <ItemTitle>Email</ItemTitle>
          <ItemDescription>Weekly summary of activity.</ItemDescription>
        </ItemContent>
      </Item>
      <ItemSeparator />
      <Item>
        <ItemMedia variant="icon">
          <Lucide.Bell />
        </ItemMedia>
        <ItemContent>
          <ItemTitle>Push</ItemTitle>
          <ItemDescription>Alerts on your devices.</ItemDescription>
        </ItemContent>
      </Item>
    </ItemGroup>
  </Box>

  ```jsx
  <ItemGroup>
    <Item>
      <ItemMedia variant="icon">
        <Mail />
      </ItemMedia>
      <ItemContent>
        <ItemTitle>Email</ItemTitle>
        <ItemDescription>Weekly summary of activity.</ItemDescription>
      </ItemContent>
    </Item>
    <ItemSeparator />
    <Item>...</Item>
  </ItemGroup>
  ```
</Example>

`ItemContent`, `ItemTitle`, `ItemActions`, `ItemGroup`, `ItemHeader` and `ItemFooter` accept
standard `div` props, and `ItemDescription` renders a `p`. `ItemHeader` and `ItemFooter` span the
full width of the item, above and below its content. Each part exposes a matching `data-slot`
attribute for styling, such as `data-slot="item"` and `data-slot="item-title"`.

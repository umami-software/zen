Shows a preview of content behind a link when the user hovers over it, for sighted mouse users. It
is built on the Base UI `PreviewCard`. Keep essential information out of a hover card, since touch
and keyboard users may never see it. Use a `Popover` for content that opens on click.

<Example>
  <HoverCard>
    <HoverCardTrigger href="https://github.com/umami-software" render={<Button variant="link" />}>
      @umami-software
    </HoverCardTrigger>
    <HoverCardContent>
      <Row gap="3">
        <Avatar>
          <AvatarImage src="https://github.com/umami-software.png" alt="umami" />
          <AvatarFallback>UM</AvatarFallback>
        </Avatar>
        <Column gap="1">
          <Text size="sm" weight="semibold">@umami-software</Text>
          <Text size="sm">Simple, fast, privacy-focused web analytics.</Text>
          <Text size="xs" color="muted">Joined July 2020</Text>
        </Column>
      </Row>
    </HoverCardContent>
  </HoverCard>

## Delay

Set `delay` and `closeDelay` on `HoverCardTrigger`, in milliseconds.

<Example>
  <HoverCard>
    <HoverCardTrigger delay={100} closeDelay={100} render={<Button variant="outline" />}>
      Hover me
    </HoverCardTrigger>
    <HoverCardContent>
      <Text size="sm">Opens after 100ms and closes 100ms after the pointer leaves.</Text>
    </HoverCardContent>
  </HoverCard>

  ```jsx
  <HoverCard>
    <HoverCardTrigger delay={100} closeDelay={100} render={<Button variant="outline" />}>
      Hover me
    </HoverCardTrigger>
    <HoverCardContent>
      <Text size="sm">Opens after 100ms and closes 100ms after the pointer leaves.</Text>
    </HoverCardContent>
  </HoverCard>
  ```
</Example>

## Side

Use `side` and `align` on `HoverCardContent` to position the card.

## Parts

- `HoverCard` is the root. It holds the open state.
- `HoverCardTrigger` is the element that opens the card on hover. It renders a link by default; use `render` to change the element.
- `HoverCardContent` is the card, rendered in a portal.

## Component props

### HoverCard

<PropertiesTable>
  <PropertiesTable.Row name="open" type="boolean">Controlled open state</PropertiesTable.Row>
  <PropertiesTable.Row name="defaultOpen" type="boolean">Initial open state when uncontrolled (default: false)</PropertiesTable.Row>
  <PropertiesTable.Row name="onOpenChange" type="(open, eventDetails) => void">Called when the card opens or closes</PropertiesTable.Row>
</PropertiesTable>

### HoverCardTrigger

<PropertiesTable>
  <PropertiesTable.Row name="delay" type="number">Milliseconds to wait before opening (default: 600)</PropertiesTable.Row>
  <PropertiesTable.Row name="closeDelay" type="number">Milliseconds to wait before closing (default: 300)</PropertiesTable.Row>
  <PropertiesTable.Row name="render" type="ReactElement | function">Replaces the rendered element</PropertiesTable.Row>
</PropertiesTable>

### HoverCardContent

<PropertiesTable>
  <PropertiesTable.Row name="side" type="string">"top" | "bottom" | "left" | "right" | "inline-start" | "inline-end" (default: "bottom")</PropertiesTable.Row>
  <PropertiesTable.Row name="align" type="string">"start" | "center" | "end" (default: "center")</PropertiesTable.Row>
  <PropertiesTable.Row name="sideOffset" type="number">Distance from the trigger in pixels (default: 4)</PropertiesTable.Row>
  <PropertiesTable.Row name="alignOffset" type="number">Offset along the alignment axis in pixels (default: 4)</PropertiesTable.Row>
</PropertiesTable>
